For Organizations & Groups

Why a CEO would invest in this for their team

Teams don't just play the game — they see themselves differently afterward.

  • Builds connection and trust across teams that carries into daily work
  • Surfaces innovative thinking by engaging different parts of the brain
  • Strengthens collaboration without a typical "training day" feel
  • Backed by neuroscience research, not just team-building trends
For Friends & Families

Why you'll want to play it together

The stories you share tonight are the ones you'll still be laughing about later.

  • Creates a genuinely safe space for honest, playful conversation
  • Story-driven format means no awkward icebreaker energy
  • Works equally well online or gathered around a table
  • Leaves people talking about it well after the session ends
